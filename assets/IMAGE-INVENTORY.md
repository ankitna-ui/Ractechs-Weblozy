1. **Seats for Train-18**: In `for weblosys.pdf` and `RACTECHS PPT V1.0.pdf` (P4), the seat icon/photo was embedded as a tiny 100×80 px thumbnail. A dedicated standalone high-resolution photograph of the Train-18 seats is missing from the source PDFs.
2. **In-Motion / Desktop Simulator**: Listed on P4 of `RACTECHS PPT V1.0.pdf` as a product line item, but only present as a miniature diagram under 150×150 px.
3. **LED Lights for Coach Interiors (`led-lights-1.png`, `led-lights-2.png`)**: In `for weblosys.pdf` (P1), the two LED light photos have a native source resolution of 183×133 px (under 200 px). While preserved at native quality, high-resolution 1080p studio photographs of the luminaire fixtures would be beneficial if higher-resolution source assets become available.
4. **Client Logos**: Extracted from presentation slides where they were rasterized. All 13 logos are clearly legible at marquee tile scale (48px height), but official SVG vector versions would provide infinite scalability.
